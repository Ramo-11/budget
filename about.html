What Sahab Budget is

Sahab Budget shows you where your money goes. You bring in a file from your bank, it sorts every purchase into a category, and you see a clear picture of each month. Then you can set budgets and savings goals and watch your progress.

  1. Import. Download a CSV file from your bank and drop it into the app.
  2. Check. We sort what we can. Fix any that look wrong and we remember your choice.
  3. Plan. Set a monthly amount for each category and see how you are doing.

Your privacy

  • Your data stays in this browser. Your transactions, budgets, and settings are saved on this device only.
  • Nothing is uploaded. There is no account, no server holding your numbers, and no tracking. Your bank file is read on your device and never sent anywhere.
  • It works without internet. After the first visit the app opens offline.

Back up and move to another device

Because your data lives in this browser, make a backup now and then. Open Settings, Backup and restore, download a backup file, and keep it somewhere safe. To move to a new phone or computer, open Sahab Budget there, go to the same place, and restore that file.

How to download a CSV from your bank

Use your bank's website, since apps often do not offer downloads. Look for Download, Export, or Statements near your list of transactions, then choose CSV. It may be called a spreadsheet or comma separated file. A few months at a time works well. If you import the same days twice, repeats are skipped. Menu names change from time to time.

Chase
Open the account and choose the download option on the activity page. Pick CSV and a date range.
Bank of America
Open the account, choose Download above the transactions, pick a CSV or spreadsheet format and dates.
Wells Fargo
Open the account, choose Download Account Activity, pick Comma Separated and dates.
Capital One
Open the account, choose Download Transactions, pick CSV and dates.
Citi
Open the account, choose Download Transactions, pick CSV and dates.
Discover
Open Activity and Statements, choose Download, pick CSV and dates.
American Express
Open Statements and Activity, choose Download, pick CSV and the details to include.
U.S. Bank
Open the account, choose Download Transactions, pick CSV and dates.
PNC
Open the account, choose Download Activity, pick CSV and dates.
Credit unions and others
Look for Download or Export on the transaction list and pick CSV.

If we do not recognize your bank's columns, the import screen asks you to point out the date, description, and amount, and remembers it for next time.

Common questions

Why is a transaction in the wrong category?

We guess from the store name, and sometimes the guess is off. Open the transaction and pick the right category. You can choose to do the same for every purchase from that store, now and in the future.

How do auto-sort rules work?

A rule is a set of words to match and a category. When a purchase contains those words, it goes into that category. Rules are created when you fix a category for a store, and you can add or edit them in Settings. A rule can also hide matching purchases, such as card payments.

What does "Hide from totals" mean?

A hidden transaction stays in your list but is left out of your spending, income, and budget numbers. Use it for things like moving money between your own accounts, so it does not count twice.

How do I track cash?

Choose Add transaction, enter the date, what it was, the amount, and a category. Cash spending then counts like any other purchase.

Can I use it offline and install it on my phone?

Yes. After your first visit it works without internet. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On a computer, look for the install button in the address bar.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your budget lives in this browser, so clearing site data deletes it. Download a backup from Settings first, and you can restore it any time.
